Logo

C is the logo in use; A and B are the other concepts drawn for it. Each is shown full size on light and dark, at favicon size (32 and 16 px) and next to the wordmark. assets/generate_mark.py draws them all.

A — Bow tie

A solid bow tie: curved wings pinched into a knot, with the pleats cut out of the cloth. The most literal reading of the name.

B — Brackets

HankoXml's < > chevrons turned inward, >•<: the same code brackets, tied into a bow tie by a knot. Keeps the family resemblance with HankoXml.

C — Lapel Current logo
